## Gain calibration with a strong and a weak station

- **A station that decodes at no gain step no longer spoils the others.** With a strong and a far, weak station on one dongle, a weak station without any RDS made every step score zero, and the calibration fell back to 20 dB, which overloads the strong one. Now such a station is left out of the choice, reported in the log (`102.8M: no RDS at any gain`) and on the node card (*ingen RDS*), and the others get their best gain.
- **With no RDS on any station** (no antenna?) the calibration uses the highest gain that does not clip, instead of a fixed 20 dB.
- **Before the first calibration**, and for the ppm measurement, the satellite uses 8.7 dB instead of 20 dB, which already overloaded a strong station.

Update as usual: `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. An existing calibration is kept; **Admin → Noder → Kalibrera om** runs a new one.
