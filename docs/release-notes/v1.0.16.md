## A single corrupted RDS group is no traffic announcement

On the morning of 28 November, 102.8 MHz showed TA on for under a second, twenty times in two hours. Each time an event was started and then discarded as too short. These were not announcements. Each was a single RDS group with a corrupted TA flag, at a time when the station was losing groups. The same corruption could also fake a TA off in the middle of a real announcement and split it in two. If that happened in the first 10 seconds, the announcement was deleted.

- **TA changes only when three groups in a row agree.** That takes under a second. The announcement is dated from the first of those groups, so nothing is lost at its start. A single group that disagrees neither starts an announcement nor ends one.
- **The log says why.** An ignored group is logged as `TA on in 1 group(s) only, ignored`, with its group type and block error rate. The 5-minute status line counts ignored groups, and the end of an announcement says in how many of its groups TA was on.

Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. Only the satellite has changed.
