## Why a traffic message was linked to Trafikverket, or not

Traffic incidents can be days apart, and Trafikverket keeps removed deviations for only 24 hours, so a miss has to be explainable afterwards. Each traffic message placed on the map now records why it was, or was not, linked to one of Trafikverket's deviations:

- **Linked:** which deviation, how far away, whether the road and the kind of incident matched, and the score.
- **Not linked:** the three nearest deviations and what stopped each: another road, too far away (and the limit), published too long after the broadcast, ended before it, planned works, another kind of incident, or neither road nor kind known on both sides.

It is in the log (`Trafikverket: incident #… linked to …` / `not linked: nearest: …`) and in the incident (`trv_note` in `/api/incidents` and `/api/v1/incidents`):

```bash
docker compose logs rds-guard | grep "Trafikverket: incident"
curl -s 'http://localhost:8022/api/incidents?hours=168' | python3 -c 'import json,sys; [print(i["label"], "|", i.get("trv_note")) for i in json.load(sys.stdin)["incidents"]]'
```

Without `TRAFIKVERKET_API_KEY` nothing is recorded or logged.

## Upgrading from v0.6.1

`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. The incidents table gets a new column by itself.
