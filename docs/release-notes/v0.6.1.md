## Trafikverket on the map: exact places, a timeline and push updates

v0.6.0 started collecting Trafikverket's traffic information. With `TRAFIKVERKET_API_KEY` set it is now put to use:

- **Exact place.** When Trafikverket reports the incident a traffic message spoke of, the pin moves to Trafikverket's point (±300 m instead of ±500 m to ±25 km) and the affected stretch of road is drawn. Radio and Trafikverket are matched by road, distance, time and kind of incident (accident, obstacle, works); planned road works are never matched to a message about an accident. It works both ways: when Trafikverket publishes after the radio, the incident is linked then.
- **A timeline.** The map popup and the event page show the radio's mentions and Trafikverket's updates in order: "Bärgning pågår", a new expected end, a changed impact, removed. The pin stays on the map while Trafikverket says the incident goes on, and turns grey when Trafikverket ends it, even if the radio never says "öppet igen".
- **Push updates.** When a traffic message was pushed by one of your rules, Trafikverket's later updates of that incident go to the same rule: *Trafik, uppdatering: E18 Bergviksmotet – Bärgning pågår. Beräknas klart 14:30.* At most every ten minutes per incident, and always when it ends. They have `event_type` `traffic_update`.
- **Trafikverket's own layer.** Tick **Trafikverkets övriga händelser** on the map to also see Trafikverket's current accidents and obstacles the radio has not mentioned, as triangles coloured by impact. Planned works and the many small traffic messages are left out. The choice is remembered in the browser.
- **MQTT:** alerts (`rds/alert`, `rds-aggregator/alert`) get a `trafikverket` field with the linked deviations, and `rds/trafikverket/update` (`rds-aggregator/trafikverket/update`) carries each update of a linked deviation.

The road number is now also read from Trafikverket's description ("Väg 924 från …") when its road number field is empty, which it often is.

All of it runs locally on the data Trafikverket publishes; no AI and no cost per message. Without the key nothing changes.

## Upgrading from v0.6.0

`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. The incidents table gets a new column by itself. Messages already on the map are not linked afterwards; new ones are.
