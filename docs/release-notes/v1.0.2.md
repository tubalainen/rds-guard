## ppm from the stations that are actually heard

- **A frequency where nothing is heard no longer sets the ppm.** With a strong station and one too weak to hear, the noise on the weak one gave a made-up frequency error (18.5 next to the real −1.7). The median of the two took it, so the dongle ran 21 ppm off. The ppm is now measured only on stations with a clear 19 kHz pilot. The log names the ones left out (`102.8M: pilot 7 dB, too weak to measure ppm on`). With two stations it takes their mean.
- **The steps checked with real decoding are picked by the stations that are heard,** so a station that is too far away no longer makes every step look equally bad.

Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, then run **Admin → Noder → Kalibrera om**. A dongle that was calibrated with such a station keeps its wrong ppm until it is calibrated again.
