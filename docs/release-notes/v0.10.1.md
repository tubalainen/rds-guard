## Old messages keep their own time on the map

After **Bygg om kartan**, some older messages linked to a Trafikverket deviation that is still current (road works, for example) were shown as just heard, under "Senaste 12 timmarna", with the time of the rebuild. Trafikverket now keeps a radio incident current only for 24 hours after the radio last spoke of it, and the map shows when the radio said it.

Incidents that already got the wrong time are given their own time back by themselves within a few minutes of the upgrade. No new rebuild is needed.

## Upgrading

`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`
