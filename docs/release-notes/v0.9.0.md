## The roads: pins on the road the announcer names

RDS Guard now knows where Sweden's numbered roads run, and the names of Trafikverket's interchanges, cameras and road weather stations. All of it is bundled in the image, so nothing is downloaded while it runs, and it costs nothing per message.

- **A road registry** (`geodata/roads-se.tsv.gz`, 1.3 MB): the 9 531 E-roads, riksvägar and länsvägar from Trafikverket's road database (NVDB). When a message names a road:
  - among places with the same name, the one next to that road is picked ("riksväg 61 vid Kärr")
  - the pin is put on the road, not beside it (at most as far as its circle reaches)
  - the direction arrow follows the road, the way that leads toward the place named ("i riktning mot Grums")
- **More place names** (`geodata/places-trafikverket.tsv.gz`, 4 611 places): Trafikverket's interchanges (trafikplatser, "Skutbergsmotet"; "Hynboholm" is also found as "Hynboholmsmotet") and the names of its traffic cameras, speed cameras and road weather stations, with their road ("Stolpen västergående" on E18 becomes Stolpen by E18). Names OpenStreetMap already has at that place are not added twice.

A road number the registry does not know near the receiver (a misheard one) changes nothing.

On the labelled set (61 messages from P4 Värmland) the rules engine finds the same incidents as before (60/77, 44 precise). Those are the messages its rules were tuned on and almost all their places were already known; the gain should show on new messages, with interchanges and camera places that were missing and places with the same name.

The registries are rebuilt with `tools/build_roads_trafikverket.py` (needs a free `TRAFIKVERKET_API_KEY` only when building), or the roads from OpenStreetMap with `tools/build_roads.py`.

Pins placed before this version stay where they are. **Admin → Karta → Bygg om kartan** places every stored message again with this version.

## Upgrading

`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`, then, if you like, Admin → Karta → Bygg om kartan.
