## Gain calibration: the steps to check are chosen by the stations that show RDS

- **A station with a pilot but no RDS no longer makes the choice random.** On docker01 (v1.0.10, Airspy R2, 100.3, 102.8 and 103.5 MHz), 100.3 had a clear 19 kHz pilot but no RDS at any gain. Its RDS signal-to-noise was about 0 at every step, so ranking the steps by the worst station was close to random. The one step where 102.8 decoded made it into the four checks only by luck. Such a station is now left out of the ranking, and the log says so (`100.3M: pilot heard but no RDS signal at any gain - left out of the ranking`).
- **Every station's own best step is always checked** with real decoding. A weak station that decodes at one gain only can no longer be left out. The other checks go to the steps where the worst of the remaining stations does best.

Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. To calibrate again with the new choice, use **Admin → Noder → Kalibrera om**.
