## Traffic announcements follow the station, not the PI

P4 switches PI during the day: the shared national programme sends 0xE224, and the regional programme sends its own PI (0xE824 on P4 Värmland). The satellite followed the TA flag separately for each PI. An announcement that started on one PI and ended on the other was never ended. It stayed open, and its recording ran to the time limit. Later announcements on that PI were not seen, because the satellite thought TA was still on. One miss could silence a station for the rest of the day. Emergency broadcasts (PTY Alarm) had the same gap.

- **TA, RadioText and PTY Alarm are followed per station (frequency), whatever PI it sends.** A station has at most one open announcement and one open emergency broadcast. A PI switch in the middle of an announcement keeps the event and its RadioText, and the event keeps the PI it started on. The log says "ended on PI …" when it ends on the other one.
- **A station that sends no RDS for 3 minutes ends what it has open**, at the time it was last heard, instead of leaving it open. When the station is heard again, its first TA value only says where things stand.

Update with `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`. Only the satellite has changed.
